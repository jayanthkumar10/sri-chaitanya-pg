Sri Chaitanya PG offers flexible configurations: Triple sharing starts from ₹8,000/month (Non-AC) and ₹10,000/month (AC). Twin sharing starts from ₹8,500/month (Non-AC) and ₹12,000/month (AC). Yes, unlimited Telugu style breakfast, lunch, and dinner are 100% included in the monthly room rent. The mess serves authentic Andhra and Telangana meals, offering residents a true taste of home-cooked food without any extra charge.
